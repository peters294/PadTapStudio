Privacy Policy – PadTap Studio

Effective date: 2026-09-09

PadTap Studio (“App”) lets users create and play custom pad scenes using their own audio files.

## 1. Data We Process

- Audio files and scene configurations selected by the user are stored locally on the device.
- We do not require account registration.

## 2. Advertising

- The App uses Google AdMob to display ads.
- AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad-related data according to Google policies:

[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

- The App uses Firebase Analytics for aggregated usage statistics (for example: feature usage and app interaction events).
- The App uses Sentry for crash and error diagnostics.
- Diagnostic/analytics data may include technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, and error stack traces.

## 4. Cloud

- Currently, user scenes and sounds are handled locally on the device.
- The App may download ready-made sound packs in the future; this policy will be updated accordingly.

## 5. Children

- The App is not directed specifically to children under 13.

## 6. Your Choices

- You can stop using the App at any time.
- You can remove local app data by uninstalling the App or clearing app storage in system settings.
